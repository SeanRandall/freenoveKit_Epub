Voice samples

Hear how each Retroreader speech engine approaches the same text at a comfortable demonstration rate.

The numerical rate scales differ between engines, so the values are not directly comparable. These samples use the same synthesis code and voice data as their corresponding firmware builds.

OpenEVV

A compact, classic screen-reader-style synthesizer and the engine from which the project began. Sample rate: 80.

Pico

A small-footprint speech engine with a smoother, more contemporary sound. Sample rate: 100.

DECtalk DTC-01

A recreation of the distinctive DECtalk family of speech familiar from assistive technology history. Sample rate: 180.

OpenBST 1998

A retro synthesizer build with a responsive, strongly synthetic character. Retroreader rate: 100.

Sample transcript

“This is the [name] speech synthesizer, speaking at rate [value]. Select me during installation to have your electronic books and documents spoken by me whilst you are out-and-about carrying the Retroreader.”
